About Us

Small crew. Big frames.

30mm Studio started with a single camera, a 30mm lens and a belief that every brand has a story worth filming properly. What began as weekend shoots for local businesses has grown into a full-service studio working with startups, D2C brands and creators across the country.

We stay deliberately lean. A tight team of directors, cinematographers, editors and designers means you always work directly with the people making your film.
